Joe GRAnd

Joe Grand, also known as Kingpin, is a computer engineer, hardware hacker, creator of the first electronic badges for DEF CON, member of L0pht Heavy Industries, and former technological juvenile delinquent. In 1998, at age 22, he testified before the US Senate about government computer security. He has created security tools, conference badges, videos, talks, and training courses that shaped hacker culture and hardware security.

Portland, OR • Grand Idea Studio • offspec.io • Discovery Channel’s Prototype This! • Honorary Doctor (UAT) — exploring electronic systems since the 1980s.
