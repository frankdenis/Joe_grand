Hardware Hacking Basics

Joe's flagship two-day, hands-on course — teardown to firmware extraction, ending with a live-ammo challenge against a custom hardware target you keep. No prior hardware experience required.

Book: Hardwear.io NL 2026 ↗ All courses at GIS ↗

Syllabus (2 days)

  • A · Overview
    Objectives, motivations, common attack vectors, methodology.
  • B · Information gathering
    OSINT resources, patents, certifications.
  • C · Product teardown
    Assembly/disassembly, anti-tamper; hands-on: defeat epoxy encapsulation.
  • D · Schematics & PCBs
    Reading and creating schematics, board construction; hands-on: modify target PCB.
  • E · Soldering & desoldering
    Tools and techniques; two hands-on exercises.
  • F · Buses & interfaces
    UART/I2C/SPI, multimeter measurements, logic analyzer decoding; hands-on: interactive console via UART.
  • G · Signal/data manipulation
    Debug interfaces (JTAG, SWD), fault injection & glitching overview.
  • H · Memory & firmware
    Hands-on: extract/modify EEPROM data; firmware analysis tools.
  • I · Final challenge
    Defeat the security mechanism of a custom electronic device.
