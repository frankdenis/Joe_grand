Short bio (100 words)

Joe Grand, also known as Kingpin, is a computer engineer, hardware hacker, teacher, honorary doctor, occasional YouTuber, member of legendary hacker group L0pht Heavy Industries, proprietor of Grand Idea Studio, and co-founder of offspec.io. He has been creating, exploring, and manipulating electronic systems since the 1980s. In 1998 he testified before the US Senate about government computer security — at age 22. He created DEF CON's first electronic badges, co-hosted Discovery Channel's Prototype This!, and showed the world how to recover $2 million from a locked crypto wallet.

Long bio

Joe Grand grew up in Boston and has been taking things apart since he was seven. In the 1990s he was a member of L0pht Heavy Industries, the hacker think tank that pioneered responsible disclosure and, on May 19, 1998, testified before the US Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs that weak security put the nation's networks at risk. Joe — testifying under his handle Kingpin, at age 22 — was the youngest of the seven.

After earning a B.S. in Computer Engineering from Boston University, he founded Grand Idea Studio (2002), a product design and security research firm in Portland, Oregon. His "Hands-on Hardware Hacking" training premiered at Black Hat in 2005 and still runs today as Hardware Hacking Basics. From 2006 to 2010 he created DEF CON's first electronic badges, launching an entire conference-badge culture, and returned in 2019 to design the DEF CON 27 badge. He co-hosted Discovery Channel's Prototype This! (2008–2009, 13 episodes), wrote Hardware Hacking: Have Fun While Voiding Your Warranty (2004), co-authored The Hardware Hacking Handbook (2021), created the open-source JTAGulator, and co-founded offspec.io for cryptocurrency wallet recovery. His 2022 video "How I hacked a hardware crypto wallet and recovered $2 million" passed six million views, and in 2025 he appeared in the feature film Cold Wallet. He holds an honorary Doctorate of Science in Technology from the University of Advancing Technology.
