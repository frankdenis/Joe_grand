Community • Policy

Code of Conduct

Applies to this fan site's community spaces — the Discord link-outs, event meetups, and any collaborative spaces run under this site. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

Our standards

  • Be kind, curious, and willing to share failures as well as wins.
  • Teach one, learn one — answer a question when you can.
  • Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
  • Respect safety first: no unvetted mains-voltage advice, PPE reminders welcome.
  • No harassment, trolling, personal or political attacks, or publishing others' private information.
  • No selling stolen credentials, dumps, or unauthorized access — ever.

Scope & enforcement

This code applies within all community spaces and when an individual officially represents the community elsewhere. Community moderators may remove, edit, or reject content and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Decisions follow a ladder: warning → temporary ban → permanent ban, applied proportionally and, where possible, explained.

Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the moderators of Joe Grand's Official Server or via email. All complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly; reporters' identities are respected where the law allows.
